Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Plain English, no legal jargon.

MyFolioPulse is a free service. We do not sell, share, or monetise your data in any way. This notice explains exactly what we collect and why.

Who We Are

MyFolioPulse is a personal finance tool that sends daily mutual fund health digests to NRI investors. It is operated as an independent project, not a registered financial entity.

We are not a financial advisor. Nothing in our reports constitutes financial advice. All data is sourced from publicly available AMFI NAV data via mfapi.in.

What We Collect

When you sign up, we collect only what we need to generate and deliver your report:

  • Your name — to personalise your report
  • Your email address — to deliver your daily digest
  • Your timezone — to deliver at 8am your local time
  • Your fund preferences — the mutual funds you choose to track (scheme codes and names only)
  • Sign-up date — for our records

We do not collect investment amounts, broker or platform details, PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, or any other financial or personal identification data.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for one purpose only:

  • To generate your personalised daily mutual fund health report
  • To email it to you at your chosen time
  • To maintain your private dashboard

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering your report.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your sign-up information is stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the MyFolioPulse system. It is never made public. Your generated dashboard is hosted on GitHub Pages at a private URL known only to you.

We use the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google Sheets — stores your sign-up data (private, not shared)
  • GitHub Pages — hosts your dashboard and this website
  • Gmail — sends your daily email report
  • mfapi.in — provides publicly available mutual fund NAV data

Who Can See Your Data

Only the MyFolioPulse system has access to your data. No third party, advertiser, or other user can see your information. Your dashboard URL is a private link — it is only ever sent to your own email address and is not indexed by search engines.

How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as you are an active subscriber. If you unsubscribe, your data is deleted from our system within 7 days including your dashboard page.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe — every email contains an unsubscribe link. Clicking it stops all future emails immediately.
  • Request deletion — email us and we will delete all your data within 7 days.
  • Request a copy — you can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you at any time.

Cookies & Tracking

MyFolioPulse does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any form of behavioural tracking on this website.

Changes to This Notice

If we make significant changes to this privacy notice, we will notify active subscribers by email before the changes take effect.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or concerns:

📧 Email us at: myfoliopulse@gmail.com
We aim to respond within 48 hours.
